💡 Essential Delhi Travel Tips

🚇 Pro Tip: Take the Delhi Metro!

The Delhi Metro is air-conditioned, incredibly safe, extremely well-connected, and features clear English signage. It's the best way to bypass road traffic.

Stay Connected

Keep Google Maps open on your phone and ensure your mobile data package is active before stepping out.

Hydration & Clothing

Dress comfortably and respectfully, especially when visiting heritage or religious sites, and carry a water bottle.

🔒 Protect Your Belongings

Keep your mobile phone, wallet and important documents safe, especially in crowded places.

🚇 Use Public Transport

Prefer Delhi Metro and official transport services for convenient travelling.

⚠️ Avoid Unknown Guides

Avoid suspicious tour guides and unknown people offering unusually cheap travel deals.

🌙 Travel Safely at Night

Prefer well-lit and populated routes. Avoid isolated areas when travelling alone.

📞 Save Emergency Numbers

Save important helpline numbers such as 112 and keep your phone charged.

🏨 Verify Hotel Bookings

Check hotel reviews, location and booking confirmation before travelling.

India Gate - Travel Information
Location:

Kartavya Path, New Delhi, India.

Nearest Metro:

Central Secretariat Metro Station (Yellow/Violet Line).

Highlights:
  • Famous India Gate War Memorial.
  • National War Memorial nearby.
  • Beautiful lawns and open spaces.
  • Attractive lighting during evening.
History:

India Gate was designed by Sir Edwin Lutyens and inaugurated in 1931. It was built as a war memorial in memory of Indian soldiers who died during World War I and the Third Anglo-Afghan War.

Best Time to Visit:

Evening is a popular time to enjoy the monument and its surroundings.

Visitor Tips:
  • Keep the area clean.
  • Carry drinking water.
  • Follow security instructions.
Lotus Temple - Travel Information
Location:

Bahapur, Kalkaji, New Delhi, India.

Nearest Metro:

Kalkaji Mandir Metro Station (Violet/Magenta Line).

Highlights:
  • Beautiful lotus-shaped architecture.
  • Peaceful environment.
  • Large green gardens.
  • Open to people of all religions.
History:

The Lotus Temple is a Bahai House of Worship. It was designed by architect Fariborz Sahba and opened to the public in 1986. Its design represents a blooming lotus flower and symbolizes unity and peace.

Best Time to Visit:

Morning or afternoon is suitable for exploring the temple and gardens.

Visitor Tips:
  • Maintain silence inside the prayer hall.
  • Follow photography rules.
  • Respect the peaceful environment.
Qutub Minar - Travel Information
Location:

Mehrauli, South Delhi, India.

Nearest Metro:

Qutab Minar Metro Station (Yellow Line).

Highlights:
  • 72.5 metre tall historical tower.
  • Beautiful red sandstone architecture.
  • Ancient Iron Pillar.
  • Alai Darwaza and surrounding monuments.
History:

Qutub Minar construction was started by Qutb-ud-din Aibak in 1199. Later, Iltutmish added more storeys. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and an important example of early Indo-Islamic architecture.

Best Time to Visit:

Morning or late afternoon is a good time to explore the monument.

Visitor Tips:
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes.
  • Carry a water bottle.
  • Follow monument security rules.
Red Fort - Travel Information
Location:

Netaji Subhash Marg, Chandni Chowk, Old Delhi, India.

Nearest Metro:

Lal Qila Metro Station (Violet Line).

Highlights:
  • Beautiful red sandstone walls.
  • Diwan-i-Aam and Diwan-i-Khas.
  • Museums and historical buildings.
  • Evening sound and light show.
History:

The Red Fort was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in 1639. It served as the main residence of Mughal emperors for many years. Today, the Prime Minister of India hoists the national flag here on Independence Day.

Best Time to Visit:

Morning or afternoon during the cooler months.

Visitor Tips:
  • Keep your ticket safe.
  • Wear comfortable shoes.
  • Check security restrictions before visiting.
Humayun's Tomb - Travel Information
Location:

Mathura Road, Nizamuddin East, New Delhi, India.

Nearest Metro:

Jangpura Metro Station (Violet Line).

Highlights:
  • Beautiful Mughal garden architecture.
  • Large central dome.
  • Char Bagh style gardens.
  • Beautiful red sandstone and marble work.
History:

Humayun's Tomb was built in 1570 for Mughal Emperor Humayun. It was commissioned by his widow, Empress Bega Begum. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and was the first major garden-tomb in the Indian subcontinent.

Best Time to Visit:

Morning or late afternoon is ideal for exploring the gardens.

Visitor Tips:
  • Explore the gardens carefully.
  • Wear comfortable shoes.
  • Do not damage historical structures.
Akshardham Temple - Travel Information
Location:

NH 24, Pandav Nagar, New Delhi, India.

Nearest Metro:

Akshardham Metro Station (Blue Line).

Highlights:
  • Beautiful traditional stone architecture.
  • Detailed carvings and sculptures.
  • Large gardens and water features.
  • Cultural exhibitions and water show.
History:

Swaminarayan Akshardham was inaugurated in 2005. It showcases Indian culture, spirituality, traditional architecture and the teachings of Bhagwan Swaminarayan.

Best Time to Visit:

Afternoon is a good time to explore the temple complex and its exhibitions.

Visitor Tips:
  • Electronic devices and cameras are restricted inside.
  • Follow the temple dress code.
  • Check the weekly closing day before visiting.
